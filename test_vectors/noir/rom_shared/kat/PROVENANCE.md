The artifact and witness were copied from the regenerated examples/test_vectors/rom_shared.
Proofs and VKs were generated by the pinned source-built BB with:

```sh
HARDWARE_CONCURRENCY=8 bb prove -b rom_shared.json -w rom_shared.gz -t noir-recursive-no-zk --write_vk --verify -o OUTPUT_POS
HARDWARE_CONCURRENCY=8 bb prove -b rom_shared.json -w rom_shared.gz -t evm-no-zk --write_vk --verify -o OUTPUT_KEC
```

Proof files contain the BB proof only; public inputs are separate in BB output.
